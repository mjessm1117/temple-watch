# Temple Watch: design (locked decisions)

You are the Jedi Council, running the Temple on Coruscant across ~3,600 years. Each roguelike run is a 10–15 hour campaign that can rewrite the Order's fate. Tone: hopeful but costly. Personal-use fan game in the browser. **Desktop first** (laptop, mouse); phone layout gets optimized later. No sound in v1.

## Story & eras
- Opening: the Council's vision of the Temple burning, millennia away. Every era is a chance to change it; history can swing far from canon.
- Eras: Old Republic (~3,600 BBY) → Ruusan/Sith wars (~1,000) → High Republic (~380–230) → Clone Wars (~22–19). Dates from memory, high confidence on broad strokes.
- Each era ends with a boss, then a time jump; choices also decide what the next era brings. Multiple endings (prevent the Sith, save the Republic, fall to darkness).
- Story: short text scenes, dialogue choices, mission briefings, bonds/rivalries/personal quests.
- Canon figures: Council members and NPCs, plus rare recruits. Yoda appears once his era arrives (born ~896 BBY). No Temple sacking.
- Archive of legends: fallen heroes from past runs return as relics, ghosts, or lore.

## Tactical
- XCOM 2 depth: square grid, 2 actions, cover, flanking, overwatch, classic hit %.
- Team turns; every unit tracks Initiative for a possible later switch.
- Jedi: Guard (deflect/reflect), Focus pool, heavy Force terrain use (throw, collapse, pull from cover).
- Darkness: personal meters feed an Order-wide meter. Subdue vs. kill → intel.
- Downed units bleed out, die, or get captured (→ rescue missions).
- Minimal UI with an info toggle. Smart generic AI. Handmade story maps, random side maps. Difficulty levels.
- Mission types: rescue/protect, investigate, assault/defend. No lightsaber duels.

## Units
- Squad of 6: 1–3 Jedi + 2–3 troops. Full XCOM growth for everyone, permadeath, aging/retirement across time jumps.
- Jedi: Padawan → Knight (Trial) → Master; paths Guardian, Consular, Sentinel, Wayseeker; master–Padawan bonds.
- Troops: Temple Guards, Republic soldiers, mixed hires (types vary by era); classes Soldier, Scout/sniper, Medic/tech, Heavy.
- Gear: saber crafting plus deep weapon/skill customization. Full recruit customization.

## Enemies
Raiders/pirates, armies/droids, dark-side users, Force horrors (rare; kill Jedi, ignore troops).

## Strategic
- XCOM 2-depth base. Wings: Council Chamber, Archives, Training Salle, Halls of Healing, Forge, Hangar, Barracks.
- 5 resources: Credits, Intel, Kyber, Senate Favor, Recruits.
- Council seats give perks AND vote; ignoring votes costs loyalty.
- Galaxy map + list view. Carries across jumps: rooms, research, legacy, reputation.

## Look
Clean icons first, evolving later.
