- 2026-10-20: Maps (Chat M2, bucket 4): new map validator checks every layout of every mission (64): every tile reachable, 2+ separate routes to every objective and enemy entry (no single choke), cover within 3 tiles everywhere. - 2026-10-20: Temple building (Chat T2, bucket 2): the Temple rail is now a cutaway — spires, Council, War Room and Hangar above ground, 9 sublevel bays below. Pick an empty bay to build a wing there (or Build drops it in the best bay; move the dig free while it is scaffolding). 8 XCOM-style adjacency links (Recovery Ward, Still Minds, Signals Library, Quartermaster Net, Shared Smithy, Saber Forms, Drill Yard, Infirmary Bunks) glow between rooms; staff either wing to double one. Each wing keeps a room log: builds, staff, admissions, promotions, deaths, captures, Years pass. Old saves auto-place.

Deeds (crits, clutch saves, captures, multi-kills…) suggest nicknames/epithets you pick or type at promotion; rename and change voice in the identity editor. Padawans auto-pair with a free Knight/Master: +10 aim within 3 tiles, and a Padawan kill gives the Master +1 Focus. 3D figures show armor tier (pads, chest plate, bulk), helmet/robe style, and scopes/barrels/elite stripes.
