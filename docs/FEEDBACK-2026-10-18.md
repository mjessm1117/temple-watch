# Jess feedback — 2026-10-18 (for 2026-10-19 chats)

Raw notes from Jess's play-test, sorted into work buckets. Owner chats: claim a bucket in `docs/STATUS.md` before starting.

## 1. Squad identity
- Each trooper must feel unique (look, voice line, trait, gear silhouette, nickname earned in play).
- Jedi get **Padawans**: paired unit, shared bonuses (spec C §7 bonds: Master+Padawan start at bond 2 — build it).
- Armor: **+1 DEF vs incoming damage until broken** (armor pips that strip on hits; Heavy's Shredder already strips 1).

## 2. Temple as a real building
- Temple = visible, growing building. Add modules/wings over time; it grows into a home base.
- Each wing adds abilities **and history** (who staffed it, what happened there).
- Ties to spec B §2 wings.

## 3. Multiple strike teams
- Grow to several teams: a Jedi leads troopers, deployed across the galaxy.
- Player picks which battles to fight personally; the rest are **auto-resolved** by the assigned team (odds shown, risk of wounds/capture).

## 4. Maps & terrain
- Still too many dead ends and blind spots. Not enough variety or strategy.
- Need: flanking routes, high/low ground, sightline lanes, cover clusters, interactables (spec A §4), destructible cover.
- Add a map validator test: no unreachable tiles, ≥2 routes to every objective, no tile with zero cover within 3.

## 5. Camera & targeting (XCOM feel)
- Selecting a unit **centers the camera on it**.
- **Drag to orbit** the camera in a circle (desktop mouse; keep phone working).
- Firing: XCOM-style **over-the-shoulder aim view** with target cycling, hit %, crit %, damage range, modifiers, and ability options at the bottom.

## 6. UI + graphics pass
- Better UI for both the battle screen and the base.
- Another graphics pass: maps, in-game look, and the cinematics.
