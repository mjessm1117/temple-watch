Source: `godot/data/units.json`, the cast and codex entries in `godot/data/story/data-saga.json` and `data-codex.json`, and the Era 2/3 rosters in `public/js/data-era2.js` and `data-era3.js`. Species, hair, clothing, and signature props below are art direction where the story does not specify them. Sabers are supplied by battle equipment, not baked into Meshy geometry.

Every figure should stand upright in A-pose, face forward, have clear hands, no weapon or base, and keep narrow fabric gaps that can survive a 5,910-triangle limit.

Sava's first caped model failed Meshy pose estimation. The selected second version has separated arms and no cape so the humanoid rig succeeds.
