# W23 soldier atlas pass

These W17 soldier atlases derive from the project's existing Trooper model and texture, originally generated by Jess under his paid private Meshy 6 plan. Source: https://www.meshy.ai/ . Existing source attribution and license information: `godot/assets/models/CREDITS.md` and `public/models/CREDITS.md`.

The retained humanoid clips originate in the Quaternius Universal Animation Library, CC0: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html . W23 repainted the existing armor value blocks and class accents, resized the full atlases to 512×512, and added simplified LOD2 meshes. No new third-party assets or Meshy calls were used.
