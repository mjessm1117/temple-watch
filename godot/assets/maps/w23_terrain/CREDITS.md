# W23 terrain atlas

Both 512×512 WebP atlases contain 256×256 downsampled diffuse and roughness tiles from these Poly Haven textures, in quadrant order (top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right):

- [Stone Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/stone_floor) by Dimitrios Savva
- [Rocks Ground 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocks_ground_02) by Rob Tuytel
- [Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt) by Charlotte Baglioni
- [Stone Tiles 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/stone_tiles_02) by Charlotte Baglioni

Source files and derived atlases: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Downloaded from Poly Haven's 1K JPG diffuse/roughness files and resized to 256×256 per quadrant. The procedural cover forms and terrain mesh are original, generated in Godot; no paid assets were used.
