# W23 unit atlas pass

The Jedi, Trooper and SithHeavy texture atlases are repaints of existing project assets. Their original meshes and texture art were generated by Jess under his paid private Meshy 6 plan. Source: https://www.meshy.ai/ . Existing source attribution and license information: `godot/assets/models/CREDITS.md` and `public/models/CREDITS.md`.

W23 changed armor value blocks and accent color in the embedded atlases and matching WebP copies, then resized the atlases to 512×512 for phone use. Meshes, skinning and animation clips were preserved. No new third-party assets or Meshy calls were used.
