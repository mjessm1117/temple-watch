# Model credits

The unit models `jedi`, `trooper`, `scout`, `sithHeavy`, and `agent`, and the props `crate` and `terminal` were generated by Jess with Meshy 6 under his private paid Meshy plan. Source: https://www.meshy.ai .

These files are copied from `public/models/` and converted from meshopt-compressed, quantized glTF to standard glTF so Godot 4.7.2 can import them. Mesh shapes, textures, rigging, and animations remain from the web assets. Conversion used `@gltf-transform/cli dequantize` 4.5.1. The original source attribution is in `public/models/CREDITS.md`.
