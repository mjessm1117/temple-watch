# Temple low wall v2

One-tile low stacked sandstone wall with tight rubble, read at ~25 units; 0.98 wide, 0.8 tall; LOD0 <=3000 triangles, LOD1 <=800, textures <=512 square.

References: docs/godot/shots/meshy1-cover_half_temple-before-close.png (existing warm carved columns); docs/godot/shots/meshy1-cover_half_temple-after.png (camera and rejected cannon silhouette).

Two or three courses, broken horizontal top, large matte blocks, faint geometric carving and gold hints. No columns, cannons, legs/supports, arches, posts, plates, upright shards or taller-than-wide forms. Exact copyable A–C prompts and texture brief in cover_half_temple_v2.json.

Budget: 80 maximum; three 20-credit previews and one 10-credit refine expected. Refine only if silhouette >=4. No rigging.
