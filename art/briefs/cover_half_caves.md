# cover_half_caves

References: `godot/assets/maps/wall_caves.glb`, `door_caves.glb`, `docs/godot/shots/wave9-caves.png` (visually inspected), temple pillar. Palette: very dark brown and charcoal rock, sparse small violet mineral patches inset into the surface.
