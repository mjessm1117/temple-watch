# obj_terminal

Mission interaction console; default battle camera ~25 units plus action close-ups; 4,000 triangle budget.

Style: original space opera design, chunky silhouette, matte broad planes. References: [temple](../../docs/godot/shots/wave9-temple.png), [ruins](../../docs/godot/shots/wave9-ruins.png), existing godot/assets/maps/terminal.glb and wall_temple.glb.

NOT: glossy surfaces, thin spindles, excessive greebles, ground plates, lettering, recognizable franchise designs or imported franchise assets.
